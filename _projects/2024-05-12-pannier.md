Unfortunately, the hooks it used to clip to my bike were made from cheap, flexible plastic -- similar to the clips in the image below: 
![old pannier clip](/assets/images/printing/old-clip.png)

This meant that the clips themselves were fragile, and there was nothing to stop the pannier from flying off of my bike when it went over a bump. When one of the clips snapped off, I decided to design replacements.

I modelled and printed two of [these hooks](/assets/files/printing/pannier/pannier%20hook%20V3.STL) and one of [these clips](/assets/files/printing/pannier/Pannier%20Clip%20Assembly.STL) out of PLA+. I installed a .25" x 1" spring in the cutouts in the clip. Then, I drilled holes in the plastic backing of the pannier and mounted the parts with screws, washers, and nuts. (M3 screws for the clip and M4 screws for the hooks).
